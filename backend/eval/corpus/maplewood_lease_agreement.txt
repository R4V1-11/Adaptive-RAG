Residential Lease Agreement — 14 Maplewood Terrace, Unit 3B (Fictional, for evaluation only)

Parties
This lease is entered into between Corwin Estates LLP ("Landlord") and the tenant named in Schedule A ("Tenant") for the residential premises at 14 Maplewood Terrace, Unit 3B.

Term
The lease term is 11 months, beginning on 1 March 2026 and ending on 31 January 2027. The tenant may renew for a further 11 months by giving written notice at least 45 days before the end date. On renewal, rent may increase by no more than 6%.

Rent and Payment
Monthly rent is INR 38,500, payable on or before the 5th day of each month by bank transfer. A late fee of INR 750 applies for each payment received after the 5th, plus INR 100 for each additional day the rent remains unpaid, capped at INR 3,000 per month. Maintenance charges of INR 2,200 per month are payable separately to the Maplewood Residents Association.

Security Deposit
The tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit equal to three months of rent (INR 1,15,500). The deposit will be returned within 21 days of the tenant vacating, after deducting any unpaid rent or the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear.

Utilities
Electricity and piped gas are billed to the tenant based on individual meters. Water charges are included in the maintenance fee. Internet service is the tenant's responsibility.

Pets
The tenant may keep a maximum of one cat or one dog under 15 kg, subject to an additional refundable pet deposit of INR 10,000. Birds, reptiles and other animals are not permitted without the landlord's written consent.

Use and Alterations
The premises shall be used solely as a private residence. Subletting is prohibited. The tenant may not paint walls, drill more than 6 holes, or install fixtures without prior written approval. Quiet hours in the building are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.

Maintenance and Repairs
The landlord is responsible for structural repairs, plumbing defects, and the replacement of major appliances (refrigerator, washing machine and water heater) provided with the unit. The tenant is responsible for minor repairs costing less than INR 1,500 per incident, such as replacing light bulbs and tap washers. Urgent repairs must be reported to the property manager, Mr. Dariusz Okafor, within 24 hours.

Termination
After a lock-in period of 4 months, either party may terminate the lease by giving 2 months of written notice. If the tenant leaves during the lock-in period, the tenant forfeits one month of rent from the security deposit.

Parking
One covered parking space, numbered P-17, is allotted to the unit. Additional parking for a second vehicle costs INR 1,800 per month, subject to availability.
